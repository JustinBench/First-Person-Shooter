TaleStorm Weapons Pack – Level 1
Overview
This package contains a set of four stylized medieval weapons and shields optimized for Unity projects. Each asset is low‑poly friendly, cleanly textured, and ready to use in fantasy or RPG environments.

Contents
Falchion – curved single‑edged sword

Short Sword – compact straight blade

Round Shield – classic circular defense

Kite Shield – tall, angular shield

Included files:

FBX/ – 3D models

Materials/ – base material setup

Textures/ – shared texture pack

Prefabs/ – Unity prefabs for drag‑and‑drop use

Scene/ – demo scene (lvl_1_Weapon Scene.unity)

Features
Clean topology, matte finish

Orthographic‑friendly design for blueprint workflows

Compatible with Unity 2021.3+ (URP/HDRP tested)

Organized folder structure for easy integration

Usage
Import the .unitypackage into your Unity project.

Drag prefabs (e.g., Falchion.prefab, KiteShield.prefab) into your scene.

Adjust materials or textures as needed for your pipeline.
